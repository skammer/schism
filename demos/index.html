Schism — resizable split-pane web components

Drag a handle to resize. Use Tab to focus a handle, then ←/→ or ↑/↓ to resize. Hold Shift for big steps, Home/End for limits, Enter to toggle a collapsible pane, F6 to cycle handles within a group.
